          How the experiment works

          All money here is pretend. Prices are real, and fees and slippage are charged like a real exchange would.


          What the bot knows

          Research on rug pulls, memecoins, day trading and long-term investing, turned into rules the bot can measure. Each rule shows how strong the evidence behind it is.

          The honest headline: no study shows a reliable edge in fast memecoin trading. The evidence is strongest on what to avoid, so most of the brain is about saying no.

          What it learned from its own trades

          After 30 finished trades of one kind, the bot starts adjusting how much it bets on that kind. Results are pulled toward the average first, so a few lucky trades can't fool it.
